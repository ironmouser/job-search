# Career Asset Crafting & Human-Centric Writing Guide

This skill codifies elite career coaching frameworks (Google XYZ formula, CAR impact model, Anti-AI linguistic rules) for all asset generation pipelines (`src/lib/generator.ts`, `src/lib/ai-examples.ts`).

---

## 1. Absolute Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

* **Zero Hallucination**: Never invent employers, dates, metrics, degrees, or certifications not present in the candidate's verified background.
* **Maximum Drift Limit**: Keyword alignment may rephrase existing experience to match target job descriptions, but must preserve factual integrity.
* **No Punctuation Crutches**: Never use em-dashes ("—" or "--") or hyphens ("-") as punctuation to separate clauses. Use commas, periods, or natural sentence transitions.
* **Banned AI Filler Words**: Eliminate cliché words such as *"thrilled"*, *"passionate"*, *"dynamic"*, *"testament to"*, *"delve"*, *"leverage"*, *"seamlessly"*, *"honed"*, or *"deeply resonates"*.
* **No Dramatic Storybook Hooks**: Never open with rhetorical or hypothetical preambles (e.g. *"Picture a team struggling..."*, *"A contractor waiting weeks..."*).

## 2. Google's XYZ Formula for Resume Bullet Points

Every bullet point must demonstrate measurable impact rather than passive job duties:

$$\textbf{Accomplished [X]} \text{ as measured by } \textbf{[Y]}, \text{ by doing } \textbf{[Z]}$$

### Transformation Examples:
* ❌ *Weak / Duty-focused*: "Responsible for managing cloud migrations and database optimization."
* 🟢 *Elite XYZ*: "Cut API latency by 45% by migrating monolithic relational queries to distributed Redis caching layers, supporting 15,000+ peak concurrent users."
* ❌ *Weak / Duty-focused*: "Helped improve customer onboarding flow for enterprise clients."
* 🟢 *Elite XYZ*: "Accelerated enterprise deal velocity by reducing client onboarding time from 14 days to 4 days through automated identity verification pipelines."

### High-Impact Action Verb Taxonomy:
* **Engineering & Architecture**: *Architected, Engineered, Optimized, Refactored, Deployed, Automated, Scaled, Integrated.*
* **Product & Execution**: *Spearheaded, Launched, Streamlined, Redesigned, Prioritized, Championed, Iterated.*
* **Operations & Scale**: *Eliminated, Accelerated, Standardized, Consolidated, Mitigated, Unblocked.*
* **Revenue & Growth**: *Increased, Expanded, Captured, Negotiated, Outperformed, Generated.*

## 3. The 3-Paragraph Narrative Cover Letter Blueprint

Cover letters must consist of **exactly three short paragraphs** without salutations or sign-offs:

### Paragraph 1: The Direct Application (2–3 sentences max)
* State the exact role and company.
* State your highest-level qualification / years of domain experience.
* Connect your core strength directly to the company's product focus using grounded phrasing (*"aligns with my background in..."*, *"connects with my work in..."*, *"fits my experience in..."*).
* *Example*: *"I am excited to apply for the Senior Software Engineer role at Stripe. With over seven years of experience architecting distributed payment systems, I specialize in eliminating transaction failure points and building fault-tolerant infrastructure that engineering teams trust."*

### Paragraph 2: Business & User Impact Narrative (CAR Framework)
* **Context / Problem**: What friction or operational bottleneck existed?
* **Action**: What decisive engineering, product, or organizational solution did you lead?
* **Result & Connection**: What was the measurable business or customer benefit, and how does that outcome solve the challenges in their job description?
* *Example*: *"In my previous role at Acme Corp, payment gateway timeouts were causing an 8% checkout abandonment rate during flash sales. By redesigning our webhook retry queue and establishing idempotent order handlers, I reduced transaction drop-offs by 92% and recovered an estimated $1.2M in annual recurring revenue. Helping teams eliminate fragility in critical revenue paths is the core driver of my work."*

### Paragraph 3: Direct Call to Action (1–2 sentences)
* Professional, confident closing requesting an interview.
* *Example*: *"I would love the opportunity to discuss how my background in distributed systems resilience can support Stripe's infrastructure roadmap. I am available to connect at your earliest convenience."*

## 4. Linguistic Naturalness & Cadence Tuning

To ensure writing sounds genuinely human and authoritative:
1. **Sentence Length Variation**: Alternate between punchy, short sentences (6–10 words) and longer explanatory sentences (15–25 words) to create a natural, conversational rhythm.
2. **Conversational Professionalism**: Write as an experienced, confident colleague speaking to a peer. Avoid overly formal corporate stiffness or sycophantic flattery.
3. **Compound Modifiers**: Standard industry terms like *"customer-centric"* and *"cross-functional"* are acceptable, but avoid stacking multiple hyphenated buzzwords in a single sentence.

## 5. High-Converting Networking Messages & Q&A

### A. LinkedIn Connection Outreach (2–3 sentences)
* Keep it direct, humble, and clear about the context:
  > *"Hi [Name], I recently applied for the [Job Title] role on your team. With my background in [Core Domain/Skill], I wanted to reach out directly to introduce myself. If there is any additional context I can provide about my experience, please let me know."*

### B. Screening Q&A Strategy
* **Direct Answer First**: Answer the exact question in sentence one.
* **Evidence Second**: Provide one concrete metric or project example.
* **Tie Back Third**: Connect the capability to the prospective team's goals.
